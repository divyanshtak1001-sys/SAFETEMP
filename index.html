Real-World Threat Scenarios

01 CRITICAL

Executive Impersonation Attack

The Threat: Attacker spoofs CEO email address requesting urgent wire transfer of $250,000 to supplier.

Spoofed Domain
Urgency Tactics
Unusual Request

Our Detection: SAFE-NET flags sender domain mismatch, analyzes writing patterns, detects social engineering tactics, and alerts finance team immediately.

Prevention:

  • Verify sender email domain carefully
  • Confirm unusual requests through alternative channels
  • Implement payment authorization procedures
  • Enable DMARC/SPF email authentication
02 HIGH

Credential Harvesting via Fake Portal

The Threat: Email with link to fake bank login portal designed to steal username and password.

Malicious URL
SSL Cert Mismatch
Phishing Form

Our Detection: AI scans URLs against known phishing databases, validates SSL certificates, analyzes landing page similarities to legitimate sites, and blocks credential entry.

Prevention:

  • Bookmark official websites
  • Check SSL certificates on login pages
  • Use password managers to avoid mistyped URLs
  • Enable 2FA/MFA for critical accounts
03 MEDIUM

Payment Fraud Detection

The Threat: Unauthorized transaction detected from user account in different geographic location within seconds.

Geo Anomaly
Timing Mismatch
New Merchant

Our Detection: AI correlates device location, transaction velocity, merchant risk profile, and spending patterns to flag anomalous activities and trigger verification.

Prevention:

  • Monitor bank statements regularly
  • Set transaction alerts
  • Use secure payment methods
  • Keep devices updated with security patches
04 MEDIUM

Social Engineering via Support Channel

The Threat: Attacker impersonates support staff to trick user into sharing sensitive information or account access.

Identity Spoofing
Social Engineering
Urgency Creation

Our Detection: AI analyzes communication patterns, verifies staff identity, detects social engineering tactics, and logs all interactions for compliance review.

Prevention:

  • Never share passwords or personal info with support
  • Verify support staff identity independently
  • Use official support channels only
  • Report suspicious requests immediately
